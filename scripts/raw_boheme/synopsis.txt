Set in the Latin Quarter of Paris around 1830, four impoverished young artists—poet Rodolfo, painter Marcello, musician Schaunard, and philosopher Colline—navigate cold attics, vibrant street cafes, fiery romance, and crushing heartbreak. When frail seamstress Mimì knocks on Rodolfo's door seeking a candle flame, an ardent passion blooms, set against Marcello and Musetta's stormy courtship and the bittersweet comedy of Bohemian camaraderie.
